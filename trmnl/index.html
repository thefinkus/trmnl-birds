Bird of the Day

Data behind the TRMNL plugin of the same name: today.json and the day's pictures for TRMNL X (landscape, portrait) and TRMNL OG (landscape, portrait). The three framings are there too, with the same suffixes: hero, closeup, quiet.

Source and setup: github.com/thefinkus/trmnl-birds.

Stickers: huashu-bird-sticker by Huashu (alchaincyf), PolyForm Shield 1.0.0. Data: IOC World Bird List 15.2 (CC BY 3.0), AVONET (Tobias et al. 2022, CC BY 4.0), Wikidata (CC0).
